# Sheet 1 — Follow-up DM

Audience: 11 connected people from Arthur's campaign. ~90% are Storylane customers.
They posted about Storylane once before. They have prior context.

---

## The message

> Hey {first_name},
>
> We're running a customer impact program at Storylane — the idea is simple: customers who've driven real results share the story. One LinkedIn post, your experience, your outcome, your words.
>
> It positions you well — your audience gets to see you moved the needle on something real. And we compensate $100 for the post.
>
> It's low-lift. We share a short resource with a prompt and a few examples to make it easy to get started.
>
> Interested?
>
> Prashil

---

## Notes

- "Customer impact program" frames this as something intentional, not a one-off ask
- The positioning angle leads — $100 follows it, not the other way around
- "Your experience, your outcome, your words" — signals low-effort, high-authenticity
- CTA is soft: "Interested?" — not asking for commitment, just a reply
- Enablement doc goes out only after they say yes
