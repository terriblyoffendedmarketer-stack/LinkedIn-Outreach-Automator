# Prompt + examples

## The prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI tool you already use. Add your raw thoughts where it says — messy is fine, full sentences or half-sentences, whatever comes out.

```
Write a LinkedIn post based on my notes below.

The post is about a result I drove or a problem I solved. It should read like 
something I'd post regardless of whether Storylane was part of the story — 
Storylane is just the tool that helped. My experience and what changed is the focus.

My notes: [paste your thoughts here]

Format:
- Hook in the first two lines: a tension, a contrast, or something that makes 
  someone stop scrolling. Don't start with "I"
- Sound like how I actually talk — not polished marketing copy
- 150–200 words
- No bullet points, no numbered lists
- One punchy closing line that lands the point
- Don't use: "game-changer", "in today's world", "excited to share", 
  "it's been a journey", "revolutionize"
```

Read the draft and ask: would I post this even if Storylane wasn't in it? If yes, you're in good shape. Edit a line or two to sound more like you, and it's done.
