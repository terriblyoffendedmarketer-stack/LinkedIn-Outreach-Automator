# The prompt

Dump your raw, unfiltered thoughts below the prompt. Messy is fine — full sentences, half-sentences, bullet points, whatever comes out. The AI does the shaping.

---

## Copy and paste this:

```
Write a LinkedIn post based on my notes below.

The post is about a problem I solved and the outcome. It should be something I'd want to post regardless of whether Storylane was in the story. Storylane is just the tool that helped — it's not the hero. The hero is the result I got or the shift I made.

My notes: [paste your thoughts here]

Format rules:
- First two lines: a hook — a tension, a contrast, something that makes someone stop. Don't start with "I"
- Sound like how I actually talk, not polished marketing copy
- 150–200 words max
- No bullet points, no numbered lists, no headers
- One punchy closing line that lands the point
- Don't use: "game-changer", "in today's world", "excited to share", "it's been a journey", "revolutionize"
```

---

## Tips

- The more specific your notes, the better the output — exact numbers, real situations, things that actually happened
- Read the draft and ask: would I post this even if Storylane wasn't in it? If yes, you're in good shape
- Edit one or two lines to sound more like you — that's what makes it land
