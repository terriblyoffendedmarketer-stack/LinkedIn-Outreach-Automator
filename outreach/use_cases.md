# Talking points by use case

Pick the one that's real for you. Use these as a starting point for your raw notes — you don't need all of it, just the parts that match your actual experience.

---

## Website demo

You added an interactive demo to your homepage, a product page, or a landing page.

**What to talk about:**
- What it was like before — high bounce rate, lots of "request a demo" clicks that went nowhere, visitors leaving without knowing what the product did
- What changed — qualified leads went up, visitors self-qualified before talking to sales, time-on-page, sign-ups, demo requests from people who already knew what they wanted
- The shift: buyers now understand the product before anyone picks up the phone

---

## Sales calls

You replaced a live product environment with a Storylane demo for calls.

**What to talk about:**
- The problem — live demos crashing at the worst moment, needing engineering support, generic walkthroughs that didn't map to the prospect's situation
- What changed — consistent experience every time, ability to show exactly the right thing, reps spending less time on setup and more time on the conversation
- The shift: every demo looks the same as the best demo you've ever done

---

## Post-call follow-up

You started sending a Storylane demo link after sales conversations.

**What to talk about:**
- What was happening before — prospects going quiet after calls, no way for them to share the product internally, deals dying in silence
- What changed — champions could forward the demo to the buying committee, engagement signals showed who was still active, deals stayed alive in rooms you weren't in
- The shift: your follow-up actually does something after you send it

---

## Pre-call warmup

You sent a demo to prospects before the first discovery or demo call.

**What to talk about:**
- What it was like before — first calls spent on basics, repeating the same intro, cold conversations with people who had no context
- What changed — prospects came in knowing the product, calls went deeper faster, more time on the real conversation
- The shift: the first call felt like the second one

---

## Personalization

You built different demos for different ICPs, segments, or named accounts.

**What to talk about:**
- The problem — one demo trying to speak to every buyer, ending up speaking to none of them
- What changed — each audience saw exactly their use case, no noise, messaging matched to what they actually cared about
- The shift: personalization at scale without engineering every time

---

## Speed

Something that used to take a long time now doesn't.

**What to talk about:**
- What that bottleneck used to look like — engineering tickets, waiting weeks for a demo update, missing campaign moments because the asset wasn't ready
- What it looks like now — a new demo in an afternoon, reacting same-day, launching with a demo that's actually ready on launch day
- The shift: your team moves at the speed the work actually requires

---

*One angle, done well, is the post. You don't need to cover everything.*
