# Monthly Content themes for LinkedIn

We're going thematic with our LinkedIn posts from March onwards. Would love to see your take on them through your lens.

The ideas below are starting points. They're written with different GTM roles in mind, so there should be something that resonates with where you sit. **But please don't feel restricted by them.** If you have a better angle from your own experience, go with that. **What matters is the theme** — not the specific idea.

## **March 2026 — Demo personalization**

**The idea that a demo shouldn't look the same for every buyer.** This spans ABM campaigns, personalised landing pages, website demos with dynamic tokens, and messaging tailored to a specific ICP. Everyone in GTM has a different stake in this.

**Some angles to consider:**

- How personalised demos change what's possible in an ABM or demand gen motion
- How PMMs can set up token-based personalisation so website visitors get a demo built around them
- Why messaging-level personalisation matters more than surface-level visual changes
- Using a personalised demo to break into a cold account or re-engage a stalled one
- How sending a personalised demo before or after a call changes the conversation
- Something cool with RepX

## **April 2026 — Deal Intelligence**

The shift from tracking "views" to tracking "revenue." This month is about closing the gap between demo engagement and the CRM. It’s no longer just about who clicked a link—it’s about which deals are moving, which stakeholders are lurking, and how demos actually influence the bottom line.

**Some angles to consider:**

- **The end of "vague" attribution:** How marketers can finally point to a specific demo session as the catalyst for a closed-won deal in their next QBR.
- **Mastering the multi-thread:** Using "Buyer Reach" to identify when a champion has successfully socialized a demo with the economic buyer or IT—before they even tell you.
- **The "Warm" Discovery Call:** How sales reps can use pre-call demo signals to skip the "intro" and dive straight into the features the prospect already spent 5 minutes exploring.
- **Data-driven Pipeline Reviews:** Why Revenue Leaders should stop asking "How do you feel about this deal?" and start asking "How many stakeholders at this account have engaged with the demo?"
- **Breaking the RevOps Bottleneck:** The impact of having CRM-synced deal data out of the box without waiting weeks for a custom integration.

**Relevant resources:** [Deal Intelligence LP](https://www.storylane.io/deal-intelligence)

## **May 2026 — Deal Intelligence - follow-up post.**

The value of interactive demos is intuitive (and dare we say, obvious): educating prospects, qualifying leads, accelerating deal cycles — but with our latest release (Deal Intelligence), you can actually prove/quantify your demos' influence on pipeline.

- **PMM** - notorious challenge to prove ROI, work is qualitative in nature, difficult to prove impact on pipeline. **Deal intelligence** feature helps with attribution to the pipeline.
- **Sales** - buying committee buy-in score, knowing who your champions are in the buying committee, what they are interested in, who is the right person to reach out to, and what context to reach out with, since we know the interest of the feature they engaged with
- **Revenue leader** - quantify growth and quantify attribution
