### **What Makes Storylane Unique**

*Unlike traditional demo tools that only work for live sales calls, Storylane demos work across your entire GTM motion—from website visitors to sales calls to customer onboarding—*eliminating redundant software in your stack

- **Multi-format flexibility:** Create HTML, screenshot, video, and sandbox demos from one platform
- **AI-powered creation:** Storylane’s AI generates scripts, voiceovers, and demo flows automatically
- **Lily AI sales agent:** Lily is an AI conversational sales agent trained on your best sales resource for automated product discovery, lead qualification, and objection handling

### Our Product Lines

1. **Demo suite - Combination of Demos and Hubs**
    1. [**Interactive Demos**](https://www.storylane.io/interactive-demos) - **Guided Demos (**Communicate product value**) + [Sandbox Demos](https://www.storylane.io/sandbox-demo) (**Personalize and run live demos**) +** 
    2. [**Hubs](https://www.storylane.io/buyer-hub) -** All-in-one deal rooms for the modern buyer
2. [**RepX](https://www.storylane.io/repx) -** Storylane’s AI agent for sales - RepX qualifies visitors through voice and text while simultaneously rendering interactive product demos in the chat window.

## Our ICP (and their challenges)

- **Marketing** - Product, Growth, and Demand Gen
- **Sales**  - AEs
- Pre-sales

### **Their challenges:**

- Most website traffic bounces without converting
- Buyers want to see the product before talking to sales, but can't
- Sales teams spend hours doing the same basic demo over and over
- Creating personalized demos for each prospect/use case takes forever
- Live product environments crash during important demos
- Engineering has to be involved to build or update demos

### **What makes them lean in:**

- “Build in 2 minutes" (speed they can't believe)
- No engineering needed (true independence)
- Customer base includes Gong, SentinelOne, HubSpot, Microsoft (peer proof)
